Informed Consent

Dear volunteer! We invite you to participate in a study dedicated to sleep disturbances, stress, and lifestyle among students. The survey is anonymous. All collected data will be used exclusively in aggregated form for scientific purposes.

Please answer the questions thoughtfully and honestly — the accuracy of the research results depends on it.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop filling out the questionnaire at any time. After reviewing the results, you can contact the researcher for a consultation by phone: +375292873195.
